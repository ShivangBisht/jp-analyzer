# Phase 8 Alpha 6 Review Management

Alpha 6 adds corpus review management without enabling export or analyzer tuning.

- Filter sentence history by judgment, lifecycle, and failure classification.
- Inspect analyzer-observed span versus reviewer-approved target.
- Diagnose candidate present/missing, boundary match, classification match, and identity comparison.
- Supersede active records while retaining immutable history.
- Show corpus integrity and aggregate review counts.
- Operational corrections remain separate and optional.
- Export and operational activation remain disabled.
